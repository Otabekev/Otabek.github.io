Personal Training

Group classes are great, but nothing beats a one-on-one session tailored entirely to your body, your goals and your schedule. Our personal trainers design custom 8, 12 or 16-week programmes with weekly check-ins, progress tracking and nutritional guidance.

Initial Assessment

Every new PT client receives a complimentary fitness assessment covering strength, mobility, cardiovascular endurance and body composition.

Custom Programme

Your trainer builds a periodised training block aligned with your specific goals, available equipment and weekly availability.

Ongoing Support

Weekly check-ins, form video reviews and direct messaging access to your trainer through the Iron Forge app.

Our Credentials

All Iron Forge trainers hold certifications from at least one of the following organisations:

NSCA

National Strength and Conditioning Association — the gold standard for strength coaches worldwide.

ACSM

American College of Sports Medicine — recognised globally for evidence-based exercise science.

Yoga Alliance

International registry for yoga teachers. Our yoga instructors hold RYT-200 or RYT-500 credentials.

KFCA

Korea Fitness Certification Authority — the leading domestic body for Korean fitness professionals.

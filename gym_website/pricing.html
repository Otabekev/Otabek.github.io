Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a joining fee?

No. We do not charge a joining fee for any of our plans.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes. You can freeze your membership for up to three months per year, free of charge.

Do you offer student or senior discounts?

Yes. Students and members aged 60 or above receive a 20% discount on all plans. Bring valid ID to the front desk.

Can I try the gym before joining?

Of course. Drop in any day this week for a free trial workout, no booking required.

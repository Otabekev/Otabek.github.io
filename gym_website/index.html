Welcome to Iron Forge Gym

Iron Forge Gym is more than a place to exercise. It is a community of people who are serious about their health, their strength and their goals. Whether you want to lose weight, build muscle, run your first marathon or simply feel better every day, we have the equipment, the coaches and the atmosphere to help you get there.

Our facility is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Members enjoy full access to over 200 pieces of premium equipment, free group classes, locker rooms with showers and friendly staff who remember your name.

Modern Equipment

Certified Trainers

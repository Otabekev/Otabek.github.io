Group Fitness Classes

Every Iron Forge membership includes unlimited access to our group classes. From explosive HIIT circuits to restorative yoga, there is something for every fitness level and goal. All sessions are led by certified instructors in purpose-built studios.

Our Classes

High Intensity

Cardio Blast

A 45-minute heart-pumping session combining treadmill intervals, rowing and jump rope to torch calories and build endurance.

Duration: 45 min  |  Level: All levels

Strength

Strength & Power

Barbell-focused progressive overload training. Build serious muscle and improve your compound lifts under expert coaching.

Duration: 60 min  |  Level: Intermediate

Recovery

Yoga & Flex

Deep stretching, breath work and mobility flows to aid recovery, reduce injury risk and improve overall range of motion.

Duration: 50 min  |  Level: All levels

High Intensity

HIIT Circuit

Station-based high-intensity intervals — kettlebells, battle ropes and body-weight drills — designed for maximum calorie burn in 40 minutes.

Duration: 40 min  |  Level: Advanced

Open Access

Open Mat

Unstructured studio time where members can work on their own programmes or ask instructors for tips and form corrections.

Duration: 60 min  |  Level: All levels

Cardio

Spin Cycle

High-energy indoor cycling to club music. Burn up to 700 kcal per session while building lower-body power and cardiovascular fitness.

Duration: 45 min  |  Level: All levels

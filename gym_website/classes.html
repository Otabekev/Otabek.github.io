Our Group Classes

Group training is one of the most fun and effective ways to stay consistent with your fitness goals. At Iron Forge Gym we offer more than 40 classes every week, led by experienced instructors who know how to push you safely. All classes are included with every membership plan.

Class Catalogue

Cardio Blast

A 45 minute high-energy class mixing running, cycling and rowing intervals. Perfect for burning calories and improving cardiovascular health.

Intensity: high

Strength & Power

Barbell based class focused on the big lifts: squat, bench press and deadlift. Build real strength with proper form.

Intensity: medium to high

Yoga & Flex

Restore flexibility, improve posture and calm the mind. A gentle class suitable for all levels, including absolute beginners.

Intensity: low
